Avanti Basu

Avanti Basu is a human genetics graduate with a specialized research background in reproductive and women’s health, focusing on polyendocrine metabolic ovarian syndrome (PMOS) and endometriosis. She founded The XX Atelier to translate complex endocrine and genetic science into sharp, accessible style intelligence—bridging the gap between medical research and thoughtful fashion design.
