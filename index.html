About & Founder

The XX Atelier is an independent editorial platform and textile-research studio dedicated to examining clothing as a biological environment. Through peer-reviewed science journalism, fabric audits, and ergonomic design critiques, we champion wardrobes that respect women’s hormonal and physical realities.

About the Founder — Avanti

Avanti is a human genetics graduate with a specialized research background in reproductive and women’s health, focusing on polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS) and endometriosis. Based in India, she founded The XX Atelier to translate complex endocrine and genetic science into sharp, accessible style intelligence—bridging the gap between medical research and thoughtful fashion design.
